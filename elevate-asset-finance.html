Transparency First

That rate you were quoted? There's margin built into it.

Most finance arrangements include a rate margin above what the lender actually charges. That margin can be thousands of dollars over the life of the loan. We don't operate that way. Our commission is fixed, disclosed, and comes from the lender.

No Surprises

Fees only when it's complex

For straightforward deals, we charge no broker fee. If your situation requires significant structuring work, we'll tell you upfront what the fee is and why before any work begins.

Insider Knowledge

We know how the industry works

We've worked inside finance departments and know exactly how margins get built into deals. We use that knowledge to protect you, not profit from you.
