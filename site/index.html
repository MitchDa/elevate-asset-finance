Questions people ask first.

Can't see yours? Start online and ask your broker, or give us a call.

Will starting online affect my credit score?

No. The online application doesn't run a credit check. We only do that later, after talking with you and getting your consent.

Why use a broker instead of my bank?

Your bank offers its own products at its own rates. We work across 30+ lenders and match you to the one that suits your situation. For vehicles and equipment, the difference between lenders can be significant.

Does it cost anything to get started?

No. Starting online or having a chat with us is free and there's no obligation. We'll walk you through all the costs of your loan before you decide.

What do I need to apply?

To start, just the basics: what you're financing, your work and income, and any current loans. Your broker will then ask for ID and documents like payslips or BAS, depending on the lender.

How long does approval take?

Straightforward deals are often approved within 24 to 48 hours of a complete application. Complex commercial deals or unusual assets can take longer. We'll keep you updated the whole way.

Can you help if my credit isn't perfect?

Often, yes. Some lenders specialise in non-standard applications. We'll be honest about what's realistic rather than waste your time.

What's a balloon payment?

A lump sum left owing at the end of the loan. It lowers your regular repayments, but you'll need to pay it, refinance it or sell the asset to clear it.
