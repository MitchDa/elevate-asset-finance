Jargon, translated

Words you'll hear, explained.

Chattel mortgage

You own the asset from day one, and the lender holds it as security until it's paid off. The most common way businesses finance vehicles and equipment.

In short: own it now, pay it off over time.

Balloon payment

A lump sum left owing at the end of the loan. It lowers your regular repayments, but you'll need to pay, refinance or sell to clear it.

In short: smaller repayments now, bigger payment at the end.

Comparison rate

A rate that rolls interest and most fees into one number, so you can compare loans fairly. A low headline rate with high fees can cost more overall.

In short: the truer cost of the loan.

Common questions

Things people always ask.

Can't see your question? Start online and ask your broker, or give us a call.

Will starting online affect my credit score?

No. The online assistant doesn't run a credit check. We only do that later, after talking with you and getting your consent.

Why use a broker instead of my bank?

Your bank offers its own products at its own rates. We work across 30+ lenders and match you to the one that suits your situation. For vehicles and equipment, the difference between lenders can be significant.

Do you charge a broker fee?

For most deals, no. We earn a commission from the lender at settlement. If your situation needs significant extra work, we'll tell you the fee before anything starts.

What do I need to apply?

To start, just the basics: what you're financing, your work and income, and any current loans. Your broker will then ask for ID and documents like payslips or BAS, depending on the lender.

How long does approval take?

Straightforward deals are often approved within 24 to 48 hours of a complete application. Complex commercial deals or unusual assets can take longer. We'll keep you updated the whole way.

Can you help if my credit isn't perfect?

Often, yes. Some lenders on our panel specialise in non-standard applications. We'll be honest about what's realistic rather than waste your time.
